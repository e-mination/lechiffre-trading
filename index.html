01 — Story

One method. Three doors.

LeChiffre is Elie Minassian’s rules-based XAUUSD method. The chart is Heikin Ashi. Fills are marked at the real price, so the picture on the screen and the fill in the trade are not the same number dressed up as each other.

Signals run on 1M, 3M, 5M, 15M, 30M, 1H, and 4H. They are posted to Telegram channels for 1M, 3M, 5M, and Fifteen+ LeChiffre — the Fifteen+ channel covers 15M, 30M, and 4H.

Membership is paid monthly or annually. Every P/L figure on this page, on the website, in the app, and in the channel cards is quoted on a 0.1 lot. Past performance does not guarantee future results. This is not financial advice.

02 — Method

The chart draws the trade.

The LeChiffre Pine Script indicator runs on the gold chart in TradingView. Entries, the session, nearby news, and closed wins stay on one screen. The source of the indicator is not in this repository. What follows is the working chart, and the paper record further down.

  • Heikin Ashi, real fills

    The candles are Heikin Ashi. The fill is printed at the real price.

  • Entries

    BUY and SELL marks sit on price, with the trade’s levels readable against the candles.

  • Session shading

    The active window is shaded, including London, so trades stay inside the hours the method uses.

  • News on the chart

    A Forex Factory table lists upcoming items. The visible rows on this screenshot include U.S. unemployment claims and revised consumer sentiment.

  • Win tags

    Closed winners are labeled WIN next to the entry that produced them.

03 — Website

Lechiffre.online

https://lechiffre.online/ is the public home of the method, hosted on Hostinger, in English and French. The line on the French homepage is LeChiffre ne cligne jamais.

The pages are the story, the method, the signal room, the plans, start here, Members & Results, and Markets & News. Members & Results embeds the live results table. Markets & News embeds TradingView widgets and the Forex Factory USD calendar.

Member login opens live signals. Visitors see the same room 60 minutes late, and only the latest 10 signals. Members get every entry as it fires.

04 — App

Signals on the phone.

The Android app is v1.0.6. Google Play closed testing is in progress. This repository does not ship an APK or an app bundle. The same room is on the web at lechiffre-signals.lechiffre.workers.dev/app/.

  • Tabs, one per timeframe

    Live XAUUSD signals with a tab for each timeframe, and a notification switch per timeframe.

  • BUY and SELL

    The buttons open the XAUUSD chart on TradingView. Orders are placed with the member’s own broker. The app does not send the order.

  • Results

    Win rate and net P/L on a 0.1 lot, for Week, Month, and All, with a row per timeframe.

  • Markets & News

    A tab for the chart and the USD calendar, the same job as the website page.

  • One device per code

    A member code unlocks one device. Codes are issued from an admin panel that is not part of this public repository.

  • Free preview

    Visitors see signals 60 minutes late, and only the latest 10. Members get every entry live, with a push when it fires.

Net P/L in the app is for a 0.1 lot. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

05 — Telegram

The bot at the door.

@LeChiffreOfficialBot is the branded front door. The menu opens LeChiffre as a Telegram mini app, and it has buttons for today’s results, USD news, the gold price, pricing, the method, and the risk disclaimer.

Language follows the Telegram client: English or French, with a switch on the menu. The bot is Python, using python-telegram-bot. It auto-restarts, and it is health-checked every hour. The bot source is not in this repository.

The signal channels are separate from the bot menu. 1M, 3M, 5M, and Fifteen+ (15M, 30M, and 4H) are where the signals are posted. A thread prepares the setup, states the trade, records the result, and closes the day with a summary.

07 — Build

From the alertto the phone.

A TradingView alert leaves the chart, hits a webhook, and the Cloudflare Worker delivers it. The phone gets a push. The web app updates. Telegram channels carry the signals. The bot is the menu in front of the web app. No addresses, keys, or server source are published here.

Signal pipeline

  1. 01

    TradingView

    Pine Script on a Heikin Ashi XAUUSD chart. When the rules hit, an alert fires.

  2. 02

    Webhook

    The alert is delivered to the signal server. The address stays off this public page.

  3. 03

    Cloudflare Worker

    The Worker is the server in the middle. It fans the signal out to the phone and the web app.

04

Android push

A notification for the timeframes the member turned on. v1.0.6, Google Play closed testing.

05

Web app

The same signal in the browser: lechiffre-signals.lechiffre.workers.dev/app/
